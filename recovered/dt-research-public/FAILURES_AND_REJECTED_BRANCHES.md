# Failures and Rejected Branches

DT treats failure as evidence rather than cleanup debris.

## Rejected as established conclusions

- DT proves replacement physics.
- Accessibility curvature is proven gravity.
- Reactionless propulsion is physically validated.
- Perfect cloaking is possible.
- Local faster-than-light travel is established.
- AI fluency proves consciousness or sentience.
- A synthetic simulation proves reality.
- One universal scalar can safely optimize civilization.
- Repetition or authority creates independent evidence.
- Correction should erase the failed branch.

## Required preservation record

Every failed branch should retain its source, version, prediction, parameters, baseline, execution state, observation, residual, adjacent damage, authority decision, and smallest discriminating next test.

Historical numerical claims remain historical assertions until their code, data, environment, and outputs are recovered and rerun.
