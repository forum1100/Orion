# External Data Obligations

No external dataset may enter a public retest without a provenance record.

## Required fields

- Dataset title, producing institution, version, and retrieval date
- Canonical download page and direct artifact identifier
- License or terms of use
- Required citation text
- Required notification, registration, or acknowledgment
- Original checksum and unmodified archival location
- Transformations, filters, exclusions, and derived checksums
- Test branches that consumed the data
- Whether redistribution is permitted
- Contact or notice receipt when required

## BBN data hold

Historical work used official BBN-related numerical material whose provider required notice and citation. The precise provider, terms, downloaded artifact, and notice record have not yet been recovered into this repository.

Until those records are reconciled:

- do not redistribute the dataset;
- do not represent its numbers as independently sourced here;
- do not execute a public-data validation run from remembered values;
- label any historical number as a source-attributed but presently unresolved claim.

Recovery of the canonical artifact and its terms is a prerequisite to promotion beyond toy or synthetic testing.
