# Distel Theory

Distel Theory (DT) combines **DRO** and **DUCT** into a relational, recursive, prediction-first research framework.

## Causal spine

Interaction changes accessible futures. That produces a relational state. Persistent relations form recoverable structure. History situates observation as perspective. A signal must become distinguishable from noise and rivals before an agency-like interpretation is considered. Every claim terminates in an observable footprint and preserved residual.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
 I[Interaction] --> C[Constraint]
 C --> S[State]
 S --> P[Persistence]
 P --> V[Perspective]
 V --> D[Distinguishability]
 D --> A[Agency]
 A --> F[Footprint]
```

## Surviving principles

- Persistence is recoverable continuity through topology deformation.
- Flourishing is recursive adaptive coherence under changing conditions.
- Correction should target the dominant fault while preserving viable structure and future capability.
- Branches must be tested alone, in pairs, in triads, and under necessary-neighbor absence.
- Prediction precedes execution; validation and interpretation remain separate.
- Failure, contradiction, residuals, and adjacent damage remain in the record.
- Brute force is rejected as a primary discovery methodology.

DT is an organizing and test framework, not a demonstrated theory of everything.
