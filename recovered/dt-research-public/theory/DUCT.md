# Distel Universal Constraint Topology (DUCT)

DUCT models how interaction changes the geometry of accessible futures.

Constraint is not automatically force, coercion, or harm. It is a declared narrowing or reshaping of a bounded possibility space.

## Working branches

- accessible-state geometry;
- constraint transfer or conservation hypotheses;
- constraint refraction;
- accessibility curvature;
- directional and temporal topology;
- domain translation with explicit non-equivalence;
- bind-and-release lifecycle: candidate → bound/active → residual → released.

Physical translations must preserve known conservation, causality, units, heat, wake, environment, and detectability constraints. DUCT does not establish a new physical force or prove gravity, propulsion, cloaking, or faster-than-light motion.
