# Distel Recursive Ontology (DRO)

DRO asks how interactions become persistent, recoverable organization across interruption, noise, repair, and change.

## Working concepts

- interaction and effective interaction;
- coherence and constraint consistency;
- persistence thresholds;
- reactivation and memory overlap;
- recursive perspective;
- repair, senescence, predation, and hysteresis;
- identity as recoverable relational organization;
- agency candidates as goal-preserving correction footprints.

A transient response is not automatically structure. A fluent output is not automatically agency. Persistence claims require declared thresholds, interruptions, rivals, and observable reactivation.

DRO remains partially formalized. Domain-specific units, observables, threshold calibration, and predictive comparisons remain incomplete.
