# Propulsion Conservation Laboratory

**Status:** experimental audit framework; no novel propulsion mechanism validated.

A propulsion proposal must identify where momentum and energy go. DT terminology cannot exempt a design from conservation accounting.

[
\Delta p_{craft}+\Delta p_{environment}+\Delta p_{wake}=0
]

[
E_{input}=\Delta E_{craft}+\Delta E_{environment}+Q_{heat}+E_{wake}.
]

## Expected input

- system boundary and reference frame;
- craft and test-stand mass;
- proposed interaction medium or exchanged field;
- input power and duration;
- predicted thrust and impulse;
- heat, vibration, exhaust, radiation, and wake channels;
- sensor resolution and calibration;
- conventional rival mechanisms;
- preregistered null and failure thresholds.

## Decision boundary

Reactionless propulsion, free energy, perfect cloaking, and local faster-than-light transfer remain rejected or unvalidated branches. A nonzero force signal must survive calibration, vibration, thermal, electromagnetic, outgassing, and environmental-exchange controls before interpretation.
