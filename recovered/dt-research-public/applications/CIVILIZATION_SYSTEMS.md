# Civilization Systems Laboratory

**Status:** experimental multivariable scenario framework; not a historical law or forecasting oracle.

Civilizations are modeled as changing networks of resources, constraints, institutions, knowledge, coordination, legitimacy, and recovery options. DT rejects a universal one-number civilization score because identical totals can conceal different topology and inherited history.

## Expected input

- scenario boundary and time horizon;
- population and resource flows;
- energy and infrastructure capacity;
- institutional constraints and coordination channels;
- knowledge preservation and transfer;
- accessibility distribution rather than only aggregate availability;
- shocks, bottlenecks, recovery routes, and excluded branches;
- rival models and observable indicators.

## Test structure

Compare a baseline with the smallest discriminating interventions. Include pair, triad, and absence tests when an intervention may depend on a neighboring institution or resource.

Outputs are scenario comparisons, sensitivity maps, residuals, and possible failure routes. They are not certain predictions about real populations.

## Failure rule

An intervention that improves a headline measure while destroying resilience, access, legitimacy, or recovery capacity is recorded as locally improved but systemically damaged.
