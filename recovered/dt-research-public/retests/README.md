# Failed-Branch Retest Program

**Status:** operational research protocol; individual outcomes remain unverified until recorded.

This directory holds successor tests of historically failed, rejected, incomplete, or unresolved DT branches. A retest never edits the historical record in `FAILURES_AND_REJECTED_BRANCHES.md`.

## Required record

Every retest must identify:

1. Historical branch and source.
2. Original claim, conditions, method, and failure.
3. Why a retest is now justified.
4. New tool, dataset, derivation, or correction.
5. Frozen prediction and rival explanation.
6. Units, assumptions, controls, and boundary conditions.
7. Smallest discriminating pair, triad, or absence test.
8. Execution artifact, code version, and random seed where applicable.
9. Observed result, residuals, adjacent harm, and null outcomes.
10. Scientific-status decision and provenance.

A newer result is successor evidence. It may challenge an earlier conclusion, but it cannot erase or silently relabel that conclusion.
