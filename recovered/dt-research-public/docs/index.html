Core research question

DT asks whether systems inherit structured future accessibility from their interaction and constraint histories in ways that predict future behavior beyond present-state variables and established models.

A successful simulation is treated as computational evidence only. Physical, biological, social, or cosmological claims require independent evidence appropriate to the domain.
