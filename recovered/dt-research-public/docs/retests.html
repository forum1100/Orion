Failed-Branch Successor Retest Program

Historical failed, weak, speculative, divergent, corrected, and unresolved states remain preserved. New evidence creates successor states; it does not rewrite the old record.

Current executed example: BBN Lithium-7

AlterBBN v2.2 was recovered, compiled, and executed as a successor test. The stock central result was Li-7/H = 5.46617×10-10. A preregistered mass-7 triad reduced Li-7/H to 3.84119×10-10, about a 29.7% reduction, short of the historical ~70.8% suppression target. The local mass-7 rate-adjustment mechanism is therefore weakened rather than promoted.

Retest rules

  • Recover the original claim and failure.
  • Freeze the prediction and rival explanation before execution.
  • Use the smallest discriminating test.
  • Preserve code, environment, data terms, hashes, nulls, and residuals.
  • Do not promote a physical claim because code ran.
