About Thomas J. Cutler

Thomas J. Cutler, also publishing under the name Thomas Distel, is the primary author of Distel Theory (DT = DRO + DUCT) and maintainer of the public Distel Theory Research architecture.

The public project emphasizes explicit scientific-status labels, preserved failures, provenance, prediction-first testing, reproducibility, and separation between computational evidence and empirical validation.

Commercial operating entity

Distel Axiom Dynamics LLC is the Florida operating company for commercial/platform work, filed September 10, 2026. That company record is kept distinct from individual research authorship and from the provenance of work created before formation.
